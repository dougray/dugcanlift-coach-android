A client who logs single-arm or single-leg work now shows each side on its own line in their lift progression. After three sessions a side you see how far apart the sides are, and after four, whether the gap is closing. Each set in the session log says which side it was.

Nothing is targeted: no goal for an imbalance and no warning about one. Volume still counts both sides.

Every estimated one-rep max chart now plots one point a day, the day's best set, rather than every set.
