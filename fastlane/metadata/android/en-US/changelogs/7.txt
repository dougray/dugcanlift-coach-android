Coach now uses the whole screen on tablets, foldables and in split screen: the tabs become a side rail, your roster sits beside the client you have open, and a client's charts, recipes, workouts and the week's plan spread across the space. Phones look the same as before.

You can now remove a client, from the client page or by holding their row in the roster. Coach asks first, and says how many logged days, planned meals and booked sessions go with them.

An open client stays open when the app restarts after a screen or display change.
