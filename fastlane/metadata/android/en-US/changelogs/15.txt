The Booked card reads properly through a screen reader. Its lines were separated by a "·" that is a comma on screen and either a character name or silence aloud, so a client's day arrived as four unrelated fragments and "L 3/3 · R 2/3" as no language at all — on the one line of the card that states something about a client's body.

Every line now has a spoken form composed beside the written one. What you asked for and what your client logged are announced together, because apart they are two lists of numbers with nothing between them. The By day / By lift chips say which is selected rather than showing it in colour alone. Nothing on screen changed, and nothing is given emphasis aloud that it does not have on the page.
